# Country Architect Diagnostic (CAD) v2.2 Math Methodology

Historically, systemic diagnostics have been hindered by a lack of rigorous, quantifiable, and reproducible mathematical modeling. CAD v2.2 formalizes implementation gap evaluation with precise, interdependent systems-analytic formulas.

---

## 1. Pillar I: Grassroots System Viability (GSV)

$$\text{GSV} = \frac{\text{Demand Reality} + \text{Delivery Infrastructure} + \text{Trust Architecture} + \text{Unit Economics}}{4}$$

Where:
- **Demand Reality (5.1):** Severity and frequency of pain points; substitute availability and market density.
- **Delivery Infrastructure (5.2):** Physical coverage, connectivity, mobile money agent reliability under constraints.
- **Trust Architecture (5.3):** Historically derived trust, fraud prevalence, legal enforcement credibility, transaction reversibility.
- **Unit Economics (5.4):** Rural agent commission splits, breakeven velocity, customer acquisition, and float replenishment margins.

---

## 2. Pillar II: Institutional Translation Capacity (ITC)

$$\text{ITC} = \frac{\text{Capital Presence} + \text{Data Legibility} + \text{Structuring Capacity} + \text{Regulatory Translation}}{4}$$

Where:
- **Capital Presence (6.1):** Sovereign/DFI alignment, local currency lending, ticket-size flexibility.
- **Data Legibility (6.2):** Traceability, auditability, standardization, digital ID presence, cost of ledger verification.
- **Structuring Capacity (6.3):** Risk packaging, securitization frameworks, cashflow pools, capital market (ESX) trading boards.
- **Regulatory Translation (6.4):** Licensing clarity, assets recognition, sandbox operation, hard currency convertibility logic.

---

## 3. Pillar III: System Dynamics (Dynamics)

System Dynamics synthesize coupling intensity, trajectory direction, FTS tractability, and failure risk into a standardized composite $0.0 - 10.0$ score:

$$\text{Dynamics} = ((\text{10} - \text{LIC}) \times 0.35) + (\text{MS\_n} \times 0.25) + (\text{FTS} \times 0.20) + (\text{SFPi} \times 0.20)$$

### 3.1 Lock Intensity Coefficient (LIC)
measures the structural coupling or "lock loop" between grassroots illegibility and institutional absence:

$$\text{LIC} = \frac{(10 - \text{GSV}) \times (10 - \text{ITC})}{10}$$

- **0.0 - 3.0:** Weak coupling. Single-sided interventions on the dominant constraint are highly effective.
- **3.0 - 6.0:** Moderate coupling. Interventions require dual-sided coordinate progress.
- **6.0 - 10.0:** High coupling (Lock State). Symmetrical locks where single-sided intervention is guaranteed to fail.

### 3.2 Directional Coupling (BUD / TDD)
Identifies the primary binding constraint and dependency direction:

- **Bottom-Up Dependency (BUD):** $10 - \text{GSV}$
- **Top-Down Dependency (TDD):** $10 - \text{ITC}$

When $TDD > BUD$, institutional translation is the binding block, and structural capital market tools must lead. When $BUD > TDD$, grassroots delivery is the block, meaning first-mile infrastructure must lead.

### 3.3 Momentum Score (MS) & Normalization ($\text{MS\_n}$)
Measures annual rate of change in composite conditions over a longitudinal delta:

$$\text{MS} = \frac{\text{Current Composite} - \text{Prior Composite}}{\Delta \text{Time (years)}}$$

Where Composite is $\frac{\text{GSV} + \text{ITC}}{2}$. The score is normalized as:

$$\text{MS\_n} = \min(10, \max(0, 5 + \text{MS}))$$

### 3.4 Friction Transition Score (FTS Floor)
Identifies the active FTS floor. Bound by five friction levels:
- Information friction (Tractability 7-9)
- Coordination friction (Tractability 5-7)
- Incentive friction (Tractability 4-6)
- Trust friction (Tractability 2-5)
- Regulatory friction (Tractability 1-4)

$$\text{FTS} = \min(\text{Active Frictions})$$

### 3.5 System Failure Probability (SFP)
Proportionate probability of system collapse within a $24$-month horizon ($0\% - 100\%$). It is converted to an inverse score:

$$\text{SFPi} = \frac{100 - \text{SFP}}{10}$$

---

## 4. Pillar IV: Architect Feasibility Layer (AFL)

$$\text{AFL} = \frac{\text{Capital Adequacy} + \text{Political Access} + \text{Execution Density} + \text{Data Capability} + \text{Trust Acquisition}}{5}$$

---

## 5. Master Index: Architect Readiness Index (ARI)

$$\text{ARI} = (\text{GSV} \times 0.35) + (\text{ITC} \times 0.35) + (\text{Dynamics} \times 0.20) + (\text{AFL} \times 0.10)$$
